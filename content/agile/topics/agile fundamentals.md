The **Agile principles** are outlined in the Agile Manifesto, which serves as a guiding framework for agile methodologies. Below is an outline of the 12 core principles:

### 2.2.1	1. **Customer Satisfaction through Early and Continuous Delivery**

- Deliver valuable software early and frequently.
- Keep the customer engaged and satisfied by addressing their needs promptly.

### 2.2.2	2. **Welcome Changing Requirements**

- Embrace changes, even late in development, as they can enhance the product's value.
- Agile processes adapt to evolving needs.

### 2.2.3	3. **Deliver Working Software Frequently**

- Release usable software at regular intervals, ranging from a couple of weeks to a few months.
- Prioritize shorter timeframes to maintain momentum and focus.

### 2.2.4	4. **Collaboration Between Business Stakeholders and Developers**

- Ensure continuous collaboration to align goals and requirements.
- Break silos by promoting communication and shared decision-making.

### 2.2.5	5. **Motivated Individuals**

- Build projects around motivated, skilled, and trusted team members.
- Provide the environment and support they need to succeed.

### 2.2.6	6. **Face-to-Face Communication**

- Use in-person conversations as the most effective way to convey information within a team.
- In modern practices, virtual meetings can substitute where necessary.

### 2.2.7	7. **Working Software is the Primary Measure of Progress**

- Focus on delivering functional software rather than relying solely on documentation or abstract milestones.

### 2.2.8	8. **Sustainable Development**

- Maintain a consistent pace that is sustainable for the team.
- Avoid burnout by balancing productivity and well-being.

### 2.2.9	9. **Technical Excellence and Good Design**

- Commit to high-quality engineering practices and sound design.
- Continuous improvement enhances agility.

### 2.2.10	10. **Simplicity**

- Optimize the amount of work not done; focus on what is truly essential.
- Avoid over-complication in processes and solutions.

### 2.2.11	11. **Self-Organizing Teams**

- Empower teams to organize their own work and processes.
- Trust them to make technical and organizational decisions.

### 2.2.12	12. **Regular Reflection and Adjustment**

- Hold retrospectives to reflect on how the team can become more effective.
- Continuously adjust processes and behaviors to improve outcomes.

These principles emphasize flexibility, collaboration, and a relentless focus on delivering value to customers while fostering a healthy team environment.
